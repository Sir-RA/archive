RE: DCIT 50 Practical Exam October 17, 2026 Saturday

To my DCIT 50 Laboratory students (2-3, 2-5, 2-6, and 2-7):

An overwhelming majority of students failed the practical exam. All batches and all sections took the same practical exam. There might have been a culture shock moment when the test was presented and everyone realized that I was serious when I said that the test was hard. I do not "curve" scores.

The test was designed to be difficult. It was meant to be a challenging task to assess your skills and grasp of the concepts we've discussed so far. No outlandishly new concept was required to complete the exam.

I will admit that there were some issues outside of the examination that contributed to the difficulty such as the PCs not booting up quickly and keyboards not working properly. Those issues I can understand.

I am not disappointed that no one finished the exam on time. I was disappointed that a lot of students were unable to even fix the compilation errors when running javac Main.java, and were unable to figure out how the program was supposed to work. I expect that basic problem solving skills would have been required for everyone to pass the first two semesters of the CS program.

I apologize for my shortcomings as your teacher. I would have done things differently if only I knew the situation better. And as an apology, I will give everyone another chance in the practical exam.

Everyone will get a chance to take another practical exam next next week. It will be a different problem, but with the same level of difficulty and time alloted. But now, you have the context of what I mean by a "hard" exam, and you have 2 weeks to prepare. Please prepare.

I will take the higher score between the two practical exams as your final score.

For those who want to bring their own peripherals during the exam (keyboard, mouse, monitor, etc.), you are allowed to do so. Using your own computer is not allowed. Same rules will apply regarding the use of IDEs. You may only use vi/m, gnome-text-editor, or nano.

For the next week, I will discuss how the practical exam would have been solved (although I encourage everyone to try it on your own first, without my or AI's assistance). Attendance is not required for both Lecture and Lab (except for 2-3 lecture since I am not the instructor for that class). This is solely for practice purposes. The new practical exam will not be easier than the previous exam, but hopefully everyone will be more capable by that time.

Computer Science is a difficult program. I want everyone to be happy and pass exams but if I lower my standards, it will just be detremental to your future as Computer Scientist. This is what it takes to be globally competitive. Go beyond. Plus Ultra.
